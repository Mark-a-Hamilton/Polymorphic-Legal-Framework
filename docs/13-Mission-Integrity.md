# **1️⃣3️⃣ Mission Integrity**

## **📘 Purpose of This Document**  
Mission Integrity explains **how polymorphic laws remain a coherent framework**, rather than a loose collection of independent laws. It describes how missions are structured, how they interact, and how the **House of Experts** ensures that every law aligns with the wider legal architecture of the UK.

---

## **🛡️ 1. Why Mission Integrity Matters**

### **🔍 The Core Question**  
“How do we know that the missions of each law will work together, rather than becoming isolated or contradictory?”

In static law, this problem is common:

- laws evolve independently  
- amendments accumulate over decades  
- contradictions emerge  
- political negotiation slows everything down  

This leads to a legal system that is **historic**, fragmented, and difficult to maintain.

### **🔧 The Polymorphic Solution**  
In polymorphic law, **Mission Integrity is actively maintained** by the House of Experts. Their role is to:

- identify the **clear path** that connects laws together  
- ensure missions do not contradict each other  
- maintain a unified legal framework  
- guarantee that every law serves the UK and its citizens efficiently  

This transforms the legal system from a **collection of laws** into a **cohesive, adaptive framework**.

---

## **🏛️ 2. What Missions Actually Contain**

Up to now, “missions” have been described generally. Here are their **three concrete components**:

### **1️⃣ Acts — The Shared Foundations**  
Acts are the **core principles** that multiple laws reference.

- An Act defines what every law must do to serve the UK and its citizens.  
- If an Act changes, **every law referencing it updates automatically**.  
- One Act change can modernise **dozens or hundreds of laws at once**.  

This is the backbone of Mission Integrity.

### **2️⃣ 🎯 Specific Missions — The Goal of the Law**  
Each law has a mission describing:

- its purpose  
- its intended outcome  
- the problem it solves  
- the benefit it delivers to the electorate  

These missions are **expert‑defined**, apolitical, and designed for maximum efficiency.

### **3️⃣ ⚖️ Punishment — Sentencing Guidelines**  
Every mission includes:

- the offence  
- the sentencing criteria  
- the proportional punishment  

This ensures consistency across the framework and prevents political manipulation of sentencing.

---

## **🔗 3. How Acts Maintain Framework‑Level Integrity**

Acts are the **shared constants** that knit the entire legal system together.

### **✔ Shorter Laws**  
Instead of repeating the same criteria in every law, each law simply references the Act.

### **✔ Faster Modernisation**  
If societal, environmental, or technological changes require an update:

- Experts update the Act  
- Every law referencing it updates instantly  
- The entire framework modernises in one step  

### **✔ Guaranteed Alignment**  
Because Acts are shared:

- contradictions are eliminated  
- laws remain interoperable  
- the framework evolves as a unified whole  

This is the structural advantage static law cannot achieve.

---

## **🚀 4. Why This Creates a True Framework (Not Just Many Laws)**

Polymorphic law becomes a **framework** because:

- Acts provide shared foundations  
- Missions provide expert‑defined goals  
- Punishment provides consistent enforcement  
- Definitions adapt to the electorate  
- Experts maintain coherence  
- Parliament resolves contradictions  
- The electorate drives environmental updates  

Together, these elements ensure:

- **continuity**  
- **coherence**  
- **efficiency**  
- **adaptability**  
- **citizen‑focused evolution**  

This is fundamentally different from static law, where each law evolves independently and slowly.
