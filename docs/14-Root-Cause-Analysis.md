# **1️⃣4️⃣ Root Cause Analysis**

## **📘 Purpose of This Document**  
Root Cause Analysis explains the **behind‑the‑scenes workflow** of the House of Experts. It describes how public requirements flow from the electorate → MPs → Parliament → House of Experts → upstream legal definitions. This ensures that the **minimum number of changes** produce the **maximum positive impact** across the entire legal framework.

---

## **🗳️ 1. Electorate → MPs: Gathering Real‑World Requirements**

The process begins with the electorate:

- Citizens identify the issues that affect their daily lives  
- MPs gather this information through constituency work  
- MPs compile these requirements into their **manifestos**  

These manifestos are **unedited**, **apolitical**, and written in **plain English**, reflecting the electorate’s real needs rather than ideological positions.

This transforms elections into **information‑gathering exercises**, not political battles.

---

## **🏛️ 2. Election → Parliament: Selecting the Mandate**

During a General Election:

- The electorate chooses the manifestos that best represent their needs  
- The winning manifestos are passed directly to Parliament  
- Parliament does **not** edit or reinterpret them  
- The most popular manifestos are **shortlisted** for implementation  

This ensures the mandate is:

- **authentic**  
- **unaltered**  
- **directly from the electorate**  

No political filtering. No ideological distortion.

---

## **🧩 3. Parliament → House of Experts: Identifying the Root Causes**

The shortlisted manifestos are then submitted to the **House of Experts**, whose job is to:

- identify the **root causes** behind each public requirement  
- map each requirement to the **most upstream legal definitions**  
- determine which definitions, if updated, will produce the **largest downstream impact**  
- ensure the smallest number of changes produce the greatest benefit  

This is the core of polymorphic efficiency:

> **Minimum modifications → Maximum impact.**

---

## **🔍 4. How Experts Identify Upstream Definitions**

Experts analyse each manifesto requirement and ask:

- Which law governs this issue?  
- Which definition inside that law controls the environment?  
- Is this definition upstream (high impact) or downstream (low impact)?  
- Can updating this definition solve multiple problems at once?  

They then:

- trace the requirement back to the **root definition**  
- propose the smallest possible change  
- ensure the change aligns with the wider legal framework  
- verify that the change does not create contradictions  

This is how polymorphic law avoids the fragmentation of static law.

---

## **🔗 5. Why Root Cause Analysis Creates a True Framework**

Root Cause Analysis ensures:

### ✔ Laws evolve together  
Upstream definitions update multiple laws simultaneously.

### ✔ Contradictions are prevented  
Experts check the entire framework before approving changes.

### ✔ Efficiency is maximised  
One upstream change can fix dozens of downstream issues.

### ✔ The electorate’s needs drive the updates  
Manifestos are the raw input; experts translate them into legal action.

### ✔ Political ideology is removed  
Experts work from facts, evidence, and efficiency — not party agendas.

This is how polymorphic law becomes a **coherent, adaptive framework**, not a collection of isolated laws.
