# **1️⃣5️⃣ Governance Flow Diagram**

## **📘 Purpose of This Document**  
This document shows the **full governance flow** of the Polymorphic Legal Framework — from electorate → MPs → Parliament → House of Experts → upstream definitions → updated legal environment → electorate. It illustrates how the system maintains continuity, efficiency, and citizen‑driven adaptation.

# **🧭 Explanation of the Governance Flow**

## **1️⃣ Electorate → MPs: Capturing Real‑World Needs**  
The process begins with the electorate identifying the issues that affect their daily lives. MPs gather this information through constituency work and convert it into **plain‑English manifestos**. These manifestos are:

- unedited  
- apolitical  
- direct reflections of citizen needs  

This transforms elections into **information‑gathering exercises**, not ideological contests.

---

## **2️⃣ MPs → Election: Selecting the Mandate**  
During the election, the electorate chooses the manifestos that best represent their needs. The winning manifestos become the **democratic mandate**. No political filtering occurs — the mandate remains exactly as the electorate expressed it.

---

## **3️⃣ Election → Parliament: Receiving the Unedited Mandate**  
Parliament receives the winning manifestos **exactly as written**. They are not rewritten, negotiated, or diluted. Parliament’s role is to:

- shortlist the most popular requirements  
- pass them directly to the House of Experts  

This ensures the mandate remains **authentic and citizen‑driven**.

---

## **4️⃣ Parliament → House of Experts: Root Cause Identification**  
The House of Experts performs **Root Cause Analysis**, identifying:

- the upstream definitions controlling each issue  
- the smallest number of changes that produce the largest impact  
- contradictions or outdated definitions  
- the most efficient path to implementation  

This is where the framework becomes **intelligent and efficient**.

---

## **5️⃣ House of Experts → Upstream Definitions: Minimum Change, Maximum Impact**  
Experts update the **most upstream definitions**, ensuring:

- one change can fix multiple downstream issues  
- the framework evolves as a unified whole  
- contradictions are prevented  
- efficiency is maximised  

This is the core advantage of polymorphic law.

---

## **6️⃣ Updated Definitions → Updated Legal System**  
Once upstream definitions are updated:

- the entire legal environment modernises  
- laws become current, efficient, and enforceable  
- outdated constraints are removed  
- the framework remains coherent  

This ensures the legal system is **never older than five years**.

---

## **7️⃣ Updated Legal System → Electorate**  
The electorate experiences:

- improved services  
- modernised laws  
- clearer enforcement  
- faster adaptation  
- citizen‑focused outcomes  

This completes the cycle and prepares the next round of updates.
