# **11️⃣🔁 initions Update Cycle**

## **📘 Purpose of This Document**  
This document explains how the **Definitions Update Cycle** enables the electorate to directly influence how the law behaves, without changing the missions or rewriting legislation.  
It describes how elections become **information‑gathering cycles**, how the PM issues **apolitical instructions**, and how the **House of Experts** updates definitions to keep the legal environment modern, relevant, and responsive.

---

## **🧭 1. What the Electorate Focuses On**

The Definitions Update Cycle begins with the electorate identifying **the real‑world issues that affect their daily lives**.

These inputs are:

- plain‑English  
- apolitical  
- aideological  
- practical  
- experience‑based  

Examples include:

- housing affordability  
- access to healthcare  
- transport reliability  
- crime impact  
- environmental pressures  
- cost‑of‑living constraints  

The electorate is not asked:

- “Which party do you support?”  
- “Which ideology do you prefer?”  

Instead, they are asked:

> **“Which real‑world problems affect you the most?”**

This transforms elections into **data‑collection cycles**, not ideological contests.

---

## **🗳️ 2. Elections Become Information‑Gathering Cycles**

Under the Reformed Governance Architecture, elections serve one core purpose:

> **To gather structured information about which definitions need updating.**

The electorate’s responses determine:

- which definitions are outdated  
- which definitions cause negative impact  
- which definitions create bottlenecks  
- which definitions distort outcomes  
- which definitions no longer reflect reality  

This means:

- elections are not about parties  
- elections are not about ideology  
- elections are not about political narratives  

They are about **identifying the future direction of the country** through definition updates.

---

## **👤 3. PM Issues Apolitical Instructions**

Once the electorate’s input is analysed, the PM issues **formal instructions** to the House of Experts.

These instructions:

- contain **no political direction**  
- contain **no ideological framing**  
- contain **no party‑based objectives**  
- contain **no narrative bias**  

They simply state:

> **“Update the definitions necessary to reduce the negative impact identified by the electorate.”**

The PM acts as a **chair**, not a political leader.

---

## **🏛️ 4. House of Experts Identifies Downstream Definitions**

The House of Experts receives the PM’s instruction and determines:

- which definitions are outdated  
- which definitions cause systemic friction  
- which definitions create negative impact  
- which definitions distort mission outcomes  
- which definitions require updating  

Experts focus on **downstream definitions** — the ones closest to real‑world impact.

This ensures:

- minimal disruption  
- maximum effect  
- rapid adaptation  
- legal continuity  
- mission stability  

Experts do **not** change missions.  
Experts update **definitions only**.

---

## **🔬 5. Technical, Not Ideological, Updates**

Experts use:

- evidence  
- modelling  
- engineering logic  
- domain knowledge  
- global context  
- measurable outcomes  

to update definitions.

This removes:

- ideology  
- political bias  
- party influence  
- media distortion  
- donor pressure  

The updates are **technical**, not political.

---

## **🔄 6. Keeping the Legal Environment Modern**

The Definitions Update Cycle ensures the legal environment is:

- modern  
- adaptive  
- responsive  
- continuously updated  
- never more than **five years old**  

The law itself remains:

- unchanged  
- stable  
- mission‑aligned  
- constitutionally intact  

Only the **definitions** evolve.

This is the core principle of polymorphic law.

---

## **⚠️ 7. Only Negatively Impactful Definitions Are Updated**

Not all definitions change.

Only definitions that:

- cause measurable harm  
- distort mission outcomes  
- create systemic bottlenecks  
- no longer reflect reality  
- negatively impact the electorate  

are updated.

This prevents unnecessary change and maintains stability.

---

## **🎯 8. Conceptual Statement**

> **The Definitions Update Cycle transforms elections into apolitical information‑gathering processes.  
> The electorate identifies real‑world problems, the PM issues apolitical instructions, and the House of Experts updates downstream definitions to reduce negative impact.  
> The law remains stable; the environment evolves.**
