# **1️⃣7️⃣📚 Glossary**

## **📘 Purpose of This Document**  
This glossary defines all core terms used throughout the **Polymorphic Legal Framework**, ensuring clarity, consistency, and easy navigation across the entire system. Each term is written in plain English and aligned with the structure of your documents.

---

# **📚 Glossary of Terms**

---

## **🧩 Act**  
A shared foundational principle referenced by multiple laws.  
Updating an Act automatically updates every law that uses it, ensuring framework‑level coherence.

---

## **🎯 Adaptive Definitions**  
The fast‑changing component of a polymorphic law.  
Definitions update based on the electorate’s mandate and expert analysis, keeping the legal environment modern.

---

## **🏛️ Authority of the Electorate**  
The democratic power citizens hold to update definitions directly (via elections) or indirectly (via contradictions that block implementation).

---

## **🧠 House of Experts**  
A body of subject‑matter specialists responsible for:

- Root Cause Analysis  
- identifying upstream definitions  
- maintaining mission integrity  
- ensuring laws work together as a framework  

They are the technical engine of the system.

---

## **📜 Mission (Constant Component)**  
The expert‑defined purpose of a law.  
Missions change slowly and only through formal parliamentary process, ensuring stability.

---

## **⚖️ Punishment**  
The sentencing guidelines associated with breaking a law.  
Included within missions to ensure consistent enforcement across the framework.

---

## **🔍 Root Cause Analysis**  
The process experts use to trace electorate requirements back to the most upstream definitions.  
This ensures minimum changes produce maximum impact.

---

## **🔗 Upstream Definition**  
A high‑impact definition that influences multiple downstream laws.  
Updating an upstream definition modernises large parts of the legal system simultaneously.

---

## **🗳️ Manifesto (Plain‑English)**  
A list of citizen‑driven requirements collected by MPs.  
Manifestos are unedited and passed directly to Parliament after elections.

---

## **🏛️ Parliament (Mandate Receiver)**  
Receives unedited winning manifestos, shortlists the most popular requirements, and resolves contradictions that block implementation.

---

## **🔁 Polymorphic Law**  
A law composed of:

- **Mission** (constant)  
- **Definitions** (adaptive)  

This structure allows laws to remain stable while adapting quickly to societal changes.

---

## **📘 Static Law**  
Traditional legislation that evolves slowly, often becoming historic, contradictory, and politically contested.

---

## **🧱 Mission Integrity**  
The structural property ensuring that missions across different laws work together as a unified framework.  
Maintained by the House of Experts through shared Acts and upstream analysis.

---

## **📈 Minimum Change, Maximum Impact**  
The core efficiency principle of the framework:  
Experts update the smallest number of upstream definitions to produce the largest downstream improvements.

---

## **🔄 Definitions Update Cycle**  
The continuous process of updating definitions based on:

- elections  
- societal changes  
- environmental changes  
- technological changes  

Ensures the legal system is never older than five years.

---

## **📡 Contradiction Report**  
A formal notification sent to Parliament when a mission cannot be implemented due to conflicting legislation.  
Triggers parliamentary action to resolve the issue.
